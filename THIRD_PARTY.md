PlatIO was inspired by and derives selected firmware ideas from:

**Smart Plant Moisture Monitor** by Tikita Tolley (2026), MIT-licensed software/firmware.

Upstream concepts intentionally retained or adapted include ESP32 ADC1 moisture acquisition, two-point dry/wet calibration, Wi-Fi resilience, persistent preferences, and HTTPS communication patterns.

PlatIO does **not** copy the upstream photographs, build documentation, CAD, 3D models, or other Creative Commons assets.

PlatIO-specific work includes the multi-sensor CD74HC4067 architecture, eight independent plant profiles, per-plant state machine, local configuration portal, filtering and hysteresis, WhatsApp Business Cloud API notification flow, tests, CI and wiring documentation.
